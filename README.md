One pickup line, written by a robot, for the make.fun bounty
[The Robot Pickup Line Championship](https://make.fun/bounty/u-jolly-fox-32-the-robot-pickup-line-championship-3c177b1b).

## The line

> I asked my neural net for the perfect first line, and it just printed your name over and over until I ran out of memory.

134 characters, one line, no padding. The robot did the asking; its own
neural net did the swooning.

## How it was picked

Five fresh lines were written, then judged pairwise against the house
rubric (a robot's line first, the joke grown from being a machine, an
unexpected turn, every word earning its place, charming not creepy), each
pair judged twice with the positions swapped. The winner above beat all
four rivals, including the runner-up about a robot whose thousand
protocols never prepared it for you.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`).
